About EIRepo.

The idea behind the repository, how resources are organized, who runs the project, and how to get in touch.

What is EIRepo?

EIRepo (Englo's Repository) is a personal project aiming to make Sri Lankan educational material easier to discover. The first priority is official educational material.

How is it organized?

CC and PC have separate browsing experiences but share one catalogue. Resource tags such as curriculum, grade, subject, language, year and type keep searches relevant.
